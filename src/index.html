A simple learning routine

  1. Check. Sit with the learner and work through one question at a time.
  2. Practise. Follow the suggested activity together.
  3. Check again. Try the alternate questions to see what they can do without help.

Use English or Hindi activities. The adult’s controls are in English.

How results work

Each task records an independent response, a response with help, or a skill not yet demonstrated. Skipped tasks stay unassessed. Progress uses the most recent check at the selected language and level. Practice is recorded separately.

These original prototype items have not been standardised, equated or validated. A few questions cannot identify a disability, establish mastery or assign a grade. Different forms, language exposure and help can change results.

Recordings and privacy

In on-device AI sessions, recordings are processed on your device and are not uploaded. Public model files download from Hugging Face and jsDelivr. Responses and AI feedback are saved privately for the requesting adult. Device-generated feedback is provisional. In adult-led reading, audio stays in the active tab unless you download it. Automatic transcription is optional and off by default; enabling it may send audio to the browser’s online speech service. Only enable it with appropriate permission. An adult must review word scores.

Saved records stay in this browser. Clearing browser data removes them. Use Saved work to export a backup. There are no child accounts or shared classroom database.

About this tool

An adult-operated instructional workspace, not a clinical diagnosis or validated placement test. The activity bank is not an official or endorsed curriculum.
